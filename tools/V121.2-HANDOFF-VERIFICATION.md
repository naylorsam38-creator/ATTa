## Bugs in v121.1 found and fixed, each with a failing test first

1. **Revision `unknown` inside the Coolify container.** Every app would have been UNVERIFIED and nothing could ever reach Coolify. This was reproduced on the unmodified v121.1 code.
2. **Job identity written to `state/apps/<app>.json`.** The loop watcher stamped a finished job's run and revision onto its own later checks.
3. **One evidence folder per app.** Each new check overwrote the pictures that an older result still pointed at.
4. **`pool.map` plus a `_synth` that needs the app's folder.** One exception outside the check aborted the whole fleet.
5. **No rerun when the revision changed**, only a mark.
6. **No hard timeout.** One hung job blocked the fleet and its report.
7. **Identity collisions merged**, so two apps shared everything.
8. **No aggregate report**, and sequential runs could happen unrecorded.
9. **`evidence.jsonl` rows had no identity.**
10. **`app_runner.py all` raised a NameError on `out`.** This is also in v121.
11. **The two version-pin tests failed.**
12. **The deployment PR's kit fix (Docker Hub mirror, multi-domain) was missing.**
